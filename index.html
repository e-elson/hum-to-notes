For the cleanest result
  • Hum on “da” or “la” so each note has a clear start.
  • Keep it under 30 seconds, in a quiet room, phone a hand’s length away.
  • On a phone, “Record a hum” opens your recorder. On a computer, record a voice memo and upload it.
